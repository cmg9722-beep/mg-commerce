## Full description

```
Today is different. I'm leaving at six.

You clock in at the parking garage at 09:00 and must reach the main entrance by
18:00. Between them are nine zones and everyone who wants a minute of your time.

■ All you do is move
Attacks fire on their own. Your hands are for dodging.
Every level, pick one of three cards and build the way you fight.

■ Every zone plays by different rules
· Cafeteria — lunch. Everyone slows, but nothing you clear counts toward quota
· Conference room — during a presentation, only work done on the marker counts
· Pantry — the printer jams. Fix it and the queued output pours out at once
· Executive floor — an audit is announced. They want more documents
· CEO's office — the last door. Past it is the lobby exit

■ Thirty weapons, six slots
Rejection stamp, all-staff notice, CC mail, spilled coffee, resignation letter,
called meeting, overtime, company messenger, corporate card, attendance log,
elevator, partition, printer, water cooler, coffee machine, wet floor sign,
room booking, junior, peer, union member…
Six of them evolve when the conditions are met. You only get six slots, so what
you leave behind is the strategy.

■ Five roles — different from the first 30 seconds
Office (balanced) · Sales (fast and wide, but frail) · Dev (build once, lasts)
· HR (four cards instead of three, but hits softer) · Field (tanky, big shoves)
One unlocks at a time as you keep clocking in.

■ Promotion is a choice
Every five levels you're offered one. Take it and your power rises — so does
today's quota. Refuse and today gets easier. There is no right answer.

■ And collective bargaining
From day 15, a way to end it without hitting the CEO at all.

■ Things you can break
Desks, partitions, water coolers, copiers, plants, chairs. Not work — release.

──────────
· Fully offline — no connection required
· No ads · No in-app purchases · No accounts
· Collects no personal information
· Korean / English
· Keyboard (WASD) and touch
```
